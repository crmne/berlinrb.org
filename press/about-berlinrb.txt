ONE PARAGRAPH

Berlin.rb is a monthly meetup for Berlin's Ruby community. We meet on the second
Tuesday of every month for talks, project show-and-tells, and time with other
people who write Ruby. It is free and open to everyone, whether you have been
writing Ruby for 15 years or you are just curious about the language. Details
and registration at berlinrb.org.


THE FACTS
